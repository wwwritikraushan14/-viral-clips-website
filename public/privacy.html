Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Adults only

This service is for adults aged 18 and over only.

What we collect

When you subscribe, we collect your name, WhatsApp number, chosen plan, and the UTR / transaction ID of your UPI payment so we can verify it. We no longer ask for your email address. If you gave one when you subscribed earlier, it stays stored with your payment record.

What we never collect

We do not collect or store your UPI PIN, bank account details, or card numbers — payment happens entirely inside your own UPI app. We do not store your raw IP address. We use no advertising or tracking data.

Why we collect it

To verify your payment, create your subscription, send your private access link on WhatsApp, and enforce one-device access and plan expiry.

One-device lock

When you first open your access link, we store a one-way hash made from your IP address and browser details. It can only be used to recognise the same device again — it cannot be reversed to reveal your IP address, and your raw IP address is never stored.

Cookies

We use three strictly-necessary cookies: one that remembers your access link on your device, one that keeps the admin logged in, and one security token that protects the admin panel from forged requests. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Who handles your data

We never sell your personal data. It is handled only by the services that run this site: Railway (hosting and database), Cloudflare R2 (video storage and database backups), Google Fonts (font files — Google sees your IP address when a page loads, as on most websites), and WhatsApp (your access link is sent to your number through WhatsApp). Email delivery providers are used only if an email address is stored on your record; new subscriptions are delivered by WhatsApp.

How long we keep it

Subscription and payment records are kept while your plan is active and afterwards so renewals can be checked; we do not auto-delete them. Database backups rotate automatically (newest 20 kept, roughly 5 days). Error logs rotate automatically (newest 100 kept) and contain only the page and error message, never personal data.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete your data — contact us on WhatsApp at +91 63629 30626. If you ask for deletion, we revoke your access immediately and remove your personal details.
