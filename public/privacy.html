Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

What we collect

When you subscribe, we collect your name, email address, chosen plan, and the UTR / transaction ID of your UPI payment so we can verify it. We do not collect or store your UPI PIN, bank account details, or card numbers — payment happens entirely inside your own UPI app.

Why we collect it

To verify your payment, create your subscription, email your private access link, and enforce one-device access and plan expiry.

Cookies

We use two strictly-necessary cookies: one that remembers your access link on your device, and one that keeps the admin logged in. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Sharing

We never sell or share your personal data with third parties, except email delivery providers used to send your access link.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete your data by replying to any email from us.
